Tutorial

Controls

Move by holding down your control, it shoots a projectile one time and then, after releasing, it turns the tank in the other direction.

Gameplay

Hit all the tanks twice so they don't have any health left. Also, try not to get hit by others.

After getting shot you'll shortly get a shield, with which you can't shoot or get shooted, so drive away fast before it disappears and you get hit again.

Have fun and play fair!
