Tutorial

Controls

Your control is a button in one of the corners, it will steer your tank.
Holding it down moves the tank in the direction it is facing.
Clicking it will shoot a projectile.

Goal

Destroy all your friends' tanks by hitting them with a bullet to win.

Have fun and play fair (don't sabotage the others by removing their fingers from the screen)
